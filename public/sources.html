Frunk modelling references

  • Tesery: Juniper frunk opening and closing — aftermarket powered mechanism, used to compare the opening and hood movement.
  • Torque Alliance: Juniper frunk interior — used to compare the tub, seal and trim. The pictured accessory mat is not standard equipment.

These third-party references are linked, not bundled. The 3D frunk is an approximate instructional reconstruction fitted to the artist model. The guide retains the manual hood procedure in Tesla's documentation.

Rear-light modelling references

  • Tesla Oracle: Juniper rear-light close-up — reference for the recessed, reflected red band and smoked corner housings.
  • Hansshow: rear-lamp comparison — its narrow outline differs from the broad Juniper reflection; the outline is not reproduced as the factory design.
  • User-supplied rear-light thumbnail — secondary comparison; original publisher unverified.

Reference images remain excluded. The viewer approximates the red light reflected onto the existing recessed surface; it does not simulate measured optics or a brake signal.

Cabin modelling references

  • Hearst: white Juniper cockpit — User-supplied white-interior reference for the steering wheel, dashboard, landscape touchscreen and covered console with dual phone pads.
  • User-supplied frontal cockpit thumbnail — User-supplied frontal cockpit comparison for wheel spokes, screen placement and console layout.
  • Tesla European manual: interior layout, steering wheel, touchscreen and drive selection.

Checked 22 September 2026. The white cabin reconstruction follows the supplied Tesla configurator photographs for upholstery, console, door panels, both seating rows and rear display; dimensions are approximate. The central screen contains an original illustrative interface, not a Tesla screenshot or an interactive vehicle control. Drive selection is on the touchscreen, with a secondary selector overhead. Reference media remain excluded.
