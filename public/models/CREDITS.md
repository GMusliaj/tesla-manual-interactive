# Model Y Juniper asset (separately licensed, never mirrored here)

This file records provenance only. A local `juniper.glb` is intentionally
ignored by Git. The public viewer references the exact upstream revision below
instead of copying the binary into this repository or `dist/`; the MIT licence
for this repository does not cover it.

- Source conversion: original 307,399-triangle OBJ normalized to metres, +Y up, -Z forward; lossless meshopt compression. Materials reconstructed from object/material names because the original MTL was missing.
- Field Guide changes: white paint and upholstery, dark wheel finish, corrected window trim and lamp-housing material assignments, presentation lighting, camera tours, spatial annotations, separated opening hood, and instructional CCS/energy-flow geometry. The charge-port opening is cut into the artist body's left-rear corner, with a dark tapered cover reconstructed from the supplied Juniper manual image; it is not a rectangular box attached to the paint. These changes follow the user-provided German 2026 Premium AWD screenshots and Tesla charging references. The asset is not factory CAD; the operating geometry is an instructional reconstruction, not measured service geometry. Exact Crossflow wheels and interior trim remain approximate; the reference gallery preserves the supplied configuration images.

Tesla reference photographs and manual illustrations are copyright Tesla, Inc. They are reproduced here for this local, personal learning/reference experience. Their source pages are linked in the interface and references.json; no Creative Commons license is asserted for Tesla imagery.

The frunk reconstruction uses the separately linked Tesery motion clip and
Torque Alliance interior photograph in `references.json` as visual references
for its rounded tub, moulded surround, weather seal and hood supports. Its
geometry is generated by application code, fitted to the artist's bonnet
boundary. The aftermarket powered mechanism and accessory mat shown by those
retailers are not presented as standard vehicle equipment. Their images remain
excluded from Git and the public build; no redistribution licence is asserted.

The rear-light adaptation corrects the imported reflector and lens materials,
preserves the original recessed geometry, and procedurally approximates the
reflected red band in evening mode. The Tesla Oracle close-up is the primary
visual reference. The supplied Hansshow outline-style lamp is recorded as a
comparison rather than reproduced as the factory design. All three reference
links are recorded in `references.json`; their image files remain excluded.
