# Model Y: 3D walkthrough research brief

**Scope checked 20 September 2026.** Tesla's German manual currently identifies itself as the European **Model Y 2025+** manual, software version **2026.26**. It is the authoritative reference for the German walkthrough; the English walkthrough uses the user-specified [English European manual](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/en_eu/), checked at the same software version. The seven lesson chapters were checked in both languages. The charging exercise now follows the supplied Tesla CCS reference: dark tapered flap above a recessed seven-contact Type 2 section and two lower DC contacts. Actual equipment must be read from the vehicle's touchscreen because availability varies by configuration, region, model year, options, and software. [German manual index](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/) · [feature availability](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-20F2262F-CDF6-408E-A752-2AD9B0CC2FD6.html)

## The walkthrough should teach these moments

| Scene | 3D focus and user action | What must be learned | Safety boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Meet the car | Orbit the exterior; select flush handle, left-rear charge port, cameras, frunk and hatch. | The charge port sits behind the left-rear tail-light flap; exterior orientation is the prerequisite for every later scene. [Exterior map](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-6C6C3944-9674-4E81-A0E8-94D60B6D87B9.html) | Do not imply every shown item is fitted. |
| 2. Get in, lock, and protect rear passengers | Press the broad end of the handle, pull; enter the cabin and tap the lock control; toggle rear child locks. | The external handle pivots out after a thumb press. The normal interior release is the button at the upper door handle. Child lock is set at `Fahrzeug > Verriegelungen`; Tesla says it should always be enabled when children ride in back. [Doors](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-7A32EC01-A17E-42CC-A15B-2E0A39FD07AB.html) | Pinch-risk callout at the handle. A departing-phone lock is not proof that the vehicle is locked; Tesla makes the driver responsible for checking. |
| 3. Cockpit orientation | Use a cutaway/clickable cabin: opening button and manual release, scroll wheels, display, physical pedals, hazard button, wireless charger/card reader and rear display. | These are the relevant physical and digital controls in the documented front and rear cabins. [Interior map](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-A5F2B9D0-E7C8-40F5-9642-58F3657B123E.html) | Make the manual release visually distinct from the normal electric opening button. |
| 4. The emergency exit memory drill | Dim the cabin and remove simulated low-voltage power. Ask the learner to lift the front manual release; then expose the rear-door pocket cover and pull its mechanical cable. | Front: lift the manual release before the window switches. Rear: remove the cover in the bottom of the door pocket and pull the mechanical release forward. [No-power doors](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-A7A60DC7-E476-4A86-9C9C-10F4A276AB8B.html) | Explicitly say: never use a manual release while moving; use it only without power or when necessary, since the window will not drop and trim/glass can be damaged. |
| 5. Charge with confidence | Walk to the left rear; open the port with cable button / screen; align and fully insert connector; read a live animated port light. | A fully inserted connector locks in place; the car parks and may condition its battery before charging begins. Green pulsing means charging; steady green means finished; red means check the touchscreen message. [Charging instructions](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-BEE08D47-0CE0-4BDD-83F2-9854FB3D578F.html) | Never force the port flap. Show the red-port-light branch as a stop-and-read-screen event, not a repair instruction. |
| 6. Park with eyes up | Shift to reverse in a safe simulated car park; compare camera visualization with an exterior 360° scene; identify a child, bike, animal, low obstacle and overhanging obstacle that may not be detected. | Tesla Vision visualizes camera-observed nearby objects; red is closer and yellow is farther. It is assistance only. [Parking assist](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-005E1645-B0F5-49A0-9C59-44828B60D184.html) | Require shoulder check and mirror check to complete the scene. Tesla specifically says it can miss children, pedestrians, cyclists, animals, moving/projecting objects, and objects outside camera range. |
| 7. Respond, do not delegate | A rain/dirty-camera overlay precedes a near-collision event; learner must brake hard and maintain pressure. A separate card introduces traffic-aware cruise control and Autosteer as optional. | In an emergency, press hard and hold; do not pump the brake. Collision warning, automatic emergency braking, and obstacle-aware acceleration can assist but are not collision prevention. [Brakes](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-3DFFB071-C0F6-474D-8A45-17BE1A006365.html) · [Collision avoidance](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-8EA7EF10-7D27-42AC-A31A-96BCE5BC0A85.html) | No scene may score the learner for trusting automation. Cameras must be clean and unobstructed; rain and faded markings can reduce performance. |
| 8. Winter and cargo reality check | Frost overlays the handles, glass, mirrors and charge port; choose scheduled/app preconditioning. Then add a bike rack to a hitch-equipped vehicle and calculate total vertical load. | Tesla recommends starting climate settings 30–45 minutes before departure; defrost also works on ice at the port. The accessory-rack vertical limit is 72 kg, including the rack, when the vehicle has the tow package. [Cold weather](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-F907200E-A619-4A95-A0CF-94E0D03BEBEF.html) · [Towing/accessories](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-A6D6CF75-C9CA-4140-97FA-341E27E3B4D8.html) | Mark the rack scene unavailable without the tow package; exceeding the specified load can cause serious damage. |

## Interaction and content rules

- **Learn by locating, then doing.** Each scene begins with an unlabelled car/cabin and ends only after the learner selects the real location and performs the documented action in a reversible simulation.
- **Use a “why it matters” reveal.** The core surprise should be spatial: charging light language, a hidden rear mechanical release, camera blind zones, and the child-lock path are more memorable as animated cause and effect than as a paragraph.
- **Treat assistance as conditional.** Tesla says parking assistance, Autosteer and interior radar can vary by region/configuration/options/software. Put a visible `Check your vehicle` state beside those scenes; do not present them as Premium Long Range AWD guarantees. [Parking-assist availability](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-005E1645-B0F5-49A0-9C59-44828B60D184.html) · [Interior radar](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-4ED40B33-6E34-4E58-B8C3-9D0810DB87D6.html)
- **Use exact Tesla menu paths in German.** The walkthrough can be German-first and use concise labels, but must preserve safety-relevant paths such as `Fahrzeug > Verriegelungen > Kindersicherung` and `Fahrzeug > Aufladen > Ladeanschluss öffnen`.
- **Avoid false confidence.** Camera and driver-assistance scenes need a persistent driver-responsibility line. In Tesla's own wording, traffic-aware cruise control is a comfort function, not a collision warning/avoidance system. [Traffic-aware cruise control](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/de_de/GUID-DA920829-F1FA-44F9-8754-6D914C524A79.html)

## Suggested build order

1. Exterior/cockpit hotspot atlas and the no-power-door memory drill.
2. Charging and parking-assist simulations, including clear stop states.
3. Braking/driver-assistance limits, then winter and tow-package branches.

This order creates a useful first-day walkthrough before adding the optional and configuration-dependent systems.
